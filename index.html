This is not a prediction tool. It surfaces stocks that match well-known technical patterns (52-week-high breakouts, volume surges, top relative strength). These patterns sometimes precede further upside — and often don't. Use as a starting list for your own research. Not financial advice.

Setup Score = 55% technicals + 35% fundamentals + 10% after-hours confirmation. Combines the composite technical ranking with revenue/EPS growth, margins, valuation (PEG), and balance-sheet strength — penalized 10 points if earnings are within 7 days (binary event risk).
